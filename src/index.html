03 · Method

Look first. Leave nothing behind.

LAYER 01SEE

Lens

Before a single lamport moves, Lens shows what a stranger could learn from the payment you are about to make, so you decide with open eyes.

LAYER 02BEND

Detour

Value is broken into pieces and sent along separate paths that never share a signer with the account you hold most in. Each piece looks like unrelated noise.

LAYER 03CLOSE

Seal

Pieces converge at an address built for this one purpose. The receiver spends freely and the chain records a clean arrival with no visible origin.
